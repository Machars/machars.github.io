TP-LINK Parental Controls Guide

Set internet time limits and website filters to protect your children online.

🎓 TP-LINK Tutorial: From Beginner to Expert Intermediate 📡 TP-LINK 2026-06-02

📑 Table of Contents

  1. 1. Preschoolers
  2. 2. Elementary
  3. 3. Teenagers

Features

  • ⏰ Time limits per device
  • 🌐 Website filtering
  • 📊 Usage monitoring

Setup

  • Login tplogin.cn
  • Advanced → Parental Controls
  • Enable
  • Add child's devices
  • Set allowed time (e.g., 6PM-9PM)
  • Configure website filters

Strategies by Age

Preschoolers

  • Whitelist mode, few educational sites
  • Max 1 hour/day

Elementary

  • Time limits after homework
  • Block gaming/video sites

Teenagers

  • Negotiate internet time
  • Filter inappropriate content
  • Relax on weekends
