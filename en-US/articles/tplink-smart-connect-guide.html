TP-LINK Smart Connect Guide

How Smart Connect auto-assigns devices to the best frequency band.

🎓 TP-LINK Tutorial: From Beginner to Expert Intermediate 📡 TP-LINK 2026-06-02

What is Smart Connect?

Auto-selects 2.4GHz or 5GHz per device with a single WiFi name.

How It Works

  • Strong signal → 5GHz
  • 2.4G-only devices stay on 2.4G
  • Load balancing between bands
  • High-bandwidth devices get 5GHz

How to Enable

  • Login tplogin.cn
  • Wireless → Smart Connect
  • Enable
  • Same SSID for both bands
  • Save
