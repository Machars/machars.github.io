TP-LINK QoS Bandwidth Management Tutorial

Allocate bandwidth for smooth gaming, buffer-free video, and fair sharing.

🎓 TP-LINK Tutorial: From Beginner to Expert Intermediate 📡 TP-LINK 2026-06-03

What is QoS?

Prioritizes important applications for smooth internet.

Setup

  • Login tplogin.cn
  • Advanced → QoS
  • Enable QoS
  • Enter total bandwidth

Priority Rules

  • Device: Gaming > Work > Phones > Guests
  • App: Gaming > Video calls > Web > Downloads

Sample Rules

PriorityDeviceGuaranteedMax
-----------------------------------
HighestGaming20MbpsUnlimited
HighWork10Mbps50Mbps
MediumPhones5Mbps20Mbps
LowDownloads2Mbps10Mbps
