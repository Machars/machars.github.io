TP-LINK WiFi Signal Optimization Guide

Router placement, channel selection, antenna positioning - boost your WiFi coverage.

🎓 TP-LINK Tutorial: From Beginner to Expert Intermediate 📡 TP-LINK 2026-06-02

📑 Table of Contents

  1. 1. 2.4GHz
  2. 2. 5GHz

Router Placement

  • Center of home
  • Elevated (1.5m+)
  • Not in corners or cabinets
  • Away from appliances

Channel Optimization

2.4GHz

  • Channels 1, 6, 11 are non-overlapping
  • Use WiFi analyzer to find clearest

5GHz

  • Many channels, auto usually works

Antenna Positioning

  • 2 antennas: one vertical, one horizontal
  • Multiple: spread evenly

Other

  • Transmit power: High (default)
  • WiFi mode: Highest standard
  • Disable legacy 802.11b
