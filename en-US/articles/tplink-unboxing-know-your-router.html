TP-LINK Router Unboxing: Getting to Know Your Router

Just got a new TP-LINK router? First, get familiar with every port, button, and LED indicator.

1. Unboxing Checklist

Open the box and verify:

  • Router × 1
  • Power Adapter × 1
  • Ethernet Cable × 1
  • Quick Installation Guide × 1

2. Understanding Ports

WAN Port

  • Usually blue or labeled "Internet"/"WAN"
  • Connects to your modem/ONT

LAN Ports

  • Usually yellow or labeled "LAN"
  • Connect wired devices (PC, NAS, TV)

Reset Pinhole

  • Hold with pin for 5-10 seconds to factory reset

3. LED Indicators

LEDStatusMeaning
----------------------
PowerSolidNormal
InternetGreenConnected
InternetOrangeNo Internet
WiFiSolidWiFi On
LANBlinkingData Transfer

4. Bottom Label Info

  • Default SSID and WiFi password
  • Management URL: tplogin.cn or 192.168.0.1
  • MAC address and serial number
